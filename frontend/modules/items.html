Fill in nutrition

The assistant estimates the calories and macros of each dish from its name, and stores them as estimates. Nothing it writes appears on your menu until you confirm the dish.

This asks your own AI provider once per dish, so it costs what that provider charges. Dishes you have already filled in by hand are skipped.

    How long a section takes

    A customer ordering online sees this on the dish, and it is what tells them the kitchen is running twenty minutes behind rather than just 'busy'. Breads come off the tawa in minutes and a biryani does not, so set it a section at a time.

    Leave this ticked to keep the times you have already set by hand. Untick it to rewrite every dish in the scope.
    Who may choose how hot

    A customer ordering online can tap mild, medium or spicy on the dishes you allow it for, and the level prints on the kitchen ticket. Turn it on for the sections your kitchen cooks to order, then correct the exceptions on the dish itself.

    Only offer it where the kitchen can really cook to order. A customer who asks for mild and gets hot is worse off than one who never asked.
